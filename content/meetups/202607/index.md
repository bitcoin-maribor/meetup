## **Podjetja iz okolice #1: BCVault**

S tem predavanjem bomo v okviru društva pričeli s predstavitvami podjetij iz okolice, ki aktivno soustvarjajo v bitcoin okolju. Prvi na programu bodo kreatorji strojne denarnice/podpisnika **BCVault**. Prihajajo iz domačega Maribora in tako nas vabijo:
**BC Vault – slovenska inovacija, ki spreminja pravila varnega shranjevanja kriptovalut.**

Kako lahko slovensko znanje postavlja nove standarde na globalnem trgu kriptovalut? Na kratkem predavanju bomo predstavili BC Vault, inovativno strojno kripto denarnico, razvito v Sloveniji, ki se od večine konkurenčnih rešitev razlikuje po svojem edinstvenem pristopu k varnosti. Spoznali bomo, zakaj BC Vault ne uporablja klasične seed phrase, kako omogoča ločeno šifriranje posameznih denarnic, varnostne kopije v šifrirani obliki ter druge tehnološke rešitve, ki uporabnikom zagotavljajo večjo varnost in nadzor nad njihovimi digitalnimi sredstvi.

Predavanje je namenjeno vsem, ki jih zanimajo kriptovalute, kibernetska varnost in slovenske tehnološke inovacije z globalnim dosegom. Pridružite se nam in odkrijte, kako slovensko podjetje s svežim razmišljanjem izziva uveljavljene igralce na področju strojnih kripto denarnic.
Dobimo se v **četrtek 23. julija, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje **NiceHash**, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.

### **ENG: Local Bitcoin Companies #1: BC Vault**

With this meetup, we are launching a new series within the Bitcoin Association of Slovenia, introducing local companies that are actively contributing to the Bitcoin ecosystem. The first guests will be the creators of the **BC Vault** hardware wallet/signing device. Based here in Maribor, they invite you to join us with the following presentation:
BC Vault – A Slovenian Innovation Redefining Cryptocurrency Security.
How can Slovenian engineering set new standards in the global cryptocurrency market?

In this presentation, we will introduce **BC Vault**, an innovative hardware wallet developed in Slovenia that stands apart from most competing solutions through its unique approach to security.
You will learn why BC Vault does not rely on a traditional seed phrase, how it enables separate encryption for individual wallets, supports encrypted backups, and incorporates other technological innovations that give users greater security and control over their digital assets.
The presentation is intended for anyone interested in cryptocurrencies, cybersecurity, and Slovenian technological innovations with global reach. Join us and discover how a Slovenian company is challenging established hardware wallet manufacturers with fresh ideas and innovative engineering.

We will meet on **Thursday, July 23, at 7:00 PM** at the café of **Hotel Betnava**, Ulica Eve Lovše 15.
The event is kindly hosted at **NiceHash's** premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.
