**Bitcoin omrežje in kako se vključiti**

Tretje srečanje Bitcoin društva Slovenije v Mariboru bo tehnično usmerjeno. Razpravljali bomo o Bitcoin omrežju in njegovih tehničnih lastnostih. Predstavili bomo, kako je nastalo in se razvijalo, ter pojasniti, zakaj je praktično neuničljivo. Bitcoin omrežje vključuje številne udeležence in med predavanjem bomo pokazali, da se lahko praktično vsakdo pridruži temu obsežnemu omrežju in sodeluje na različne načine.

Srečanje bo **23\. novembra 2023 ob 19h** v kavarni hotela **Betnava (Ulica Eve Lovše 15, Maribor)**
Predavanje je namenjeno vsem, ne glede na predznanje. Ni vstopnine, vendar se prosimo prijavite, saj je število mest omejeno! Srečanje bo potekalo v slovenščini. Srečanje gosti **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenij**e in podjetje **NiceHash**.

Nato se bomo družili v kavarni, kjer bo mogoče plačati z uporabo ligtning bitcoina. Za tiste ki bodo želeli bomo v pomoč pri prvi uporabi Bitcoin LightNing (LN) tehnologije in uporabi LN denarnice, Podjetje NiceHash bo omogočilo nakup prvih satoshijev (najmanjša vrednost bitcoina).
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate [tukaj](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/) prav tako se lahko včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.

Več o bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) ali slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

**Bitcoin network and how to get involved**

The third Bitcoin Society of Slovenia meeting in Maribor will have a technical focus. We will discuss the Bitcoin network and its technical features. We will present how it was created and evolved and explain why it is practically indestructible. The Bitcoin network involves many participants, and during the lecture, we will show that practically anyone can join this extensive network and participate in various ways.

The meeting will take place on November 23rd, 2023, at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava (Eve Lovše Street 15, Maribor). Meetup is suitable for all audiences. No entrance fee, but please sign up on the MEETUP as the number of spots is limited!

**Meetup will be hosted in slovenian language.**

Meetup is hosted by **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije** and company **NiceHash**.

Afterwards we will socialize in the café, where it will be possible to pay for drinks using bitcoin.

For those who wish, we will provide assistance with their first use of Bitcoin Lightning (LN) technology and LN wallets. NiceHash company will enable the purchase of the first satoshis (the smallest unit of Bitcoin).

If you want to support us and enable future Bitcoin events you can donate [here](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/).

If you want to know more, you can read our blog on [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) or on Slo section [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
