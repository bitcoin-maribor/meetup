**Vprašanja udeležencev**
Na 15. meetupu skupine Bitcoin Maribor se bomo ponovno poslužili oblike **vprašanj iz publike**. Tako da dragi ljubitelji, vabljeni, da postanete sokreatorji tematike. Za lažjo postavljanje vprašanj smo ustvarili odprt dokument. Vanj vnesite vaša vprašanja oziroma tematike za pogovor.
[https://board.net/p/BDS-Maribor-2025-01-15-QA](https://board.net/p/BDS-Maribor-2025-01-15-QA)

Srečanje se bo odvilo v **sredo**, **15\. januarja\, ob 19h v kavarni Hotela Betnava** (ulica Eve Lovše 15, Maribor).

Pridružite se nam in razširite svoje znanje ter izkušnje v svetu napredne digitalne valute!
Nato se bomo družili v kavarni, kjer bo mogoče plačati z uporabo bitcoina.

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate [tukaj ](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/)prav tako se lahko včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na BITCOIN.SI ([https://bitcoin.si](https://bitcoin.si/)) ali slovenskem delu EUROPEAN BITCOINERS ([https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/))

Z nami bodo tudi ljudje iz bitcoin shopa. V kolikor bi kdo želel kupiti kakšno strojno denarnico ali drug produkt iz njihove trgovine, je seveda možnost da si produkt pripelje dostavit v MB. Bitcoin shop: Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila - Bitcoin Shop ([https://bitcoin-shop.si](https://bitcoin-shop.si/))

**Questions from the participants**
At the 11th meetup of the Bitcoin Maribor group, we will try something new. We will take questions from the participants of the meetup. For that we have set up a form, to take the questions:
[https://board.net/p/BDS-Maribor-2025-01-15-QA](https://board.net/p/BDS-Maribor-2025-01-15-QA)
After the event, we will gather at a café where you can meet other members of the Slovenian Bitcoin community and try out how Bitcoin payments work.

The meeting will take place on **Wednesday, January 15th, at 7 PM at the café in Hotel Betnava** (Eve Lovše Street 15, Maribor).

Meetup is hosted by Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije and company NiceHash.
If you want to support us and enable future Bitcoin events you can donate [here](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/).
If you want to know more, you can read our blog on BITCOIN.SI ([https://bitcoin.si](https://bitcoin.si/)) or on Slo section EUROPEAN BITCOINERS ([https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/)) .
