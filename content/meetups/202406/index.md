**Genealogija blockchain tehnologije**

Na **10\. meetupu** skupine **Bitcoin Maribor** se bomo posvetili tehnologiji blockchain. Klemen bo naredil pregled njenega nastanka in razvoja skozi čas ter pokazal, kako se je pomen tega pojma danes izpridil. Tehnologija veriženja blokov se vse od nastanka bitcoina spopada z medijskim direndajem in se je sprevrgla v modno besedo, s katero se označuje vsak nov tehnološki projekt, ki potrebuje rizični kapital s strani angelskih vlagateljev. S tem razlogom bomo poskusili ločiti njen izvorni pomen od današnje širše uporabe, da lažje najdemo iglo v kopici sena.

Po dogodku se bomo podružili v kavarni, kjer se boste lahko spoznali s preostalimi člani slovenske bitcoin skupnosti in preizkusili, kako delujejo bitcoin plačila preko omrežja lightning.

Srečanje se bo odvilo v četrtek, **27\. junija\, ob 19h v kavarni Hotela Betnava** (ulica Eve Lovše 15, Maribor).
Pridružite se nam in razširite svoje znanje ter izkušnje v svetu napredne digitalne valute!

Nato se bomo družili v kavarni, kjer bo mogoče plačati z uporabo ligtning bitcoina. Za tiste ki bodo želeli bomo v pomoč pri prvi uporabi Bitcoin LightNing (LN) tehnologije in uporabi LN denarnice

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate [tukaj](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/) prav tako se lahko včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.

Več o bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) ali slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

**Genealogy of Blockchain Technology**

At the **10th meetup of the Bitcoin Maribor** group, we will focus on blockchain technology. Klemen will provide an overview of its origins and development over time and demonstrate how the meaning of this concept has been corrupted today. Since the inception of Bitcoin, blockchain technology has been embroiled in media frenzy and has turned into a buzzword used to label any new technological project that needs venture capital from angel investors. For this reason, we will attempt to distinguish its original meaning from today's broader use, to better find the needle in the haystack.

After the event, we will gather at a café where you can meet other members of the Slovenian Bitcoin community and try out how Bitcoin payments work through the lightning network.

The meeting will take place on **Thursday, June 27, at 7 PM at the café in Hotel Betnava** (Eve Lovše Street 15, Maribor).

Meetup is hosted by **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije** and company **NiceHash**.

If you want to support us and enable future Bitcoin events you can donate [here](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/).

If you want to know more, you can read our blog on [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) or on Slo section [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
