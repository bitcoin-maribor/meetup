**Kako do prvega bitcoina in kako ga hraniti,**

Ker je prvi nakup bitcoina ter njegova nadaljna hramba pri večini začetnikov zelo stresna izkušnja, smo se odločili, da tokrat začnemo povsem na začetku. Danilo nam bo na naslednjem meetupu predstavil kako najlažje kupiti prve satoshije (delčki bitcoina) ter kako jih varno hraniti. Pregledali bomo kaj vse je potrebno storiti ob prvem nakupu, kje je najenostavneje, kako ga opravimo najceneje in kje ga opravimo najhitreje. Seveda je po nakupu potrebno sredstva varno spraviti in tudi to bo tema tokratnega meetupa.

Januarski meetup namenjen popolnim začetnikom, ki šele vstopajo v svet bitcoina in jim nakup povzroča težave. Seveda so vabljeni tudi bolj izkušeni, da bomo lahko izmenjali mnenja, ideje in izkušnje. 🤷‍♂️

Srečanje bo **25.1.2024 ob 19h** v kavarni hotela **Betnava (Ulica Eve Lovše 15, Maribor)**

Predavanje je namenjeno vsem, ne glede na predznanje. Ni vstopnine, vendar se prosimo prijavite, saj je število mest omejeno! Srečanje bo potekalo v slovenščini. Srečanje gosti **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenij**e in podjetje **NiceHash**.

Nato se bomo družili v kavarni, kjer bo mogoče plačati z uporabo ligtning bitcoina. Za tiste ki bodo želeli bomo v pomoč pri prvi uporabi Bitcoin LightNing (LN) tehnologije in uporabi LN denarnice.

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate [tukaj](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/) prav tako se lahko včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.

Več o bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) ali slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

**How to get the first bitcoin and how to store it,**

Since the first purchase of bitcoin and its subsequent storage can be a very stressful experience for most beginners, we have decided to start from the very beginning this time. At the next meetup Danilo will present how to easily buy the first satoshis (fractions of bitcoin) and how to safely store them. We will go through what needs to be done during the first purchase, how to buy it and where to buy it. Of course, after the purchase, it is necessary to securely store the funds, and that will be also the topic of this month meetup.

This meetup is aimed at complete beginners who are just entering the world of bitcoin and are facing difficulties with the purchase.The presentation will feature SLO slides, with the spoken portion delivered in Slovene.

The meeting will take place **25.1.2024, at 7:00 PM** at the café of Hotel Betnava (Eve Lovše Street 15, Maribor). Meetup is suitable for all audiences. No entrance fee, but please sign up on the MEETUP as the number of spots is limited!

**Meetup will be hosted in slovenian language.**

Meetup is hosted by **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije** and company **NiceHash**.

Afterwards we will socialize in the café, where it will be possible to pay for drinks using bitcoin.

If you want to support us and enable future Bitcoin events you can donate [here](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/).

If you want to know more, you can read our blog on [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) or on Slo section [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
