# 26\. november 2026 \- BDS MB meetup \#35\- Pogovor: burnout zaradi bitcoina?

## Open-mic: burnout zaradi bitcoina?

Tokrat bomo imeli odprto debato o tem kako lahko bitcoin vpliva na naše mentalno počutje. Delili bomo izkušnje s tem kako se spopasti ob izgubi ključev, spremljanju novic, nihanju cene  in podobnih dogodkih.

Dobimo se v **četrtek 26. novembra, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje **NiceHash**, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://bitcoin.si/) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si/)

### ENG: November 26, 2026 – BDS MB Meetup #35 – Open-mic: burnout with bitcoin

## Open-mic: burnout with bitcoin

This time we will have an open mic session about how bitcoin can affect our mental state. We will share and discus what situations have happened to us and how we tackle them.

We will meet on **Thursday, November 26, at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava, Ulica Eve Lovše 15.**

The event is kindly hosted at **NiceHash's** premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.

If you wish to support us and enable future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of the society and support our efforts with a **membership fee**.

Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](http://bitcoin.si/) or on the Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

Our friends from **Bitcoin Shop** will also be joining us – if you’d like to buy a **hardware wallet** or any other Bitcoin-related product, it can be conveniently picked up in Maribor.
