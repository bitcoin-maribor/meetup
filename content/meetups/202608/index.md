**27\. avgust 2026 \- BDS MB meetup \#32 \- Sledenje gotovini**

Tokrat bomo pogledali kako fiat sistem sledi gotovini, za katero zaenkrat smatramo kot najbolj anonimno. Pogledali bomo tehnologije, ki vsaki dan skenirajo gotovino in kaj vse lahko iz teh podatkov izhaja. Predstavitev pripravlja član društva.

Dobimo se v **četrtek 27. avgusta, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje NiceHash, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.

Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI ](https://bitcoin.si)ali na slovenskem delu[ EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz Bitcoin Shopa – če bi kdo želel kupiti strojno denarnico ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.

➡️ [Bitcoin Shop](https://bitcoin-shop.si/) – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila

**Novica iz marca 2026**
V mescu marcu 2026 smo po dobrih dveh letih znotraj organiziranja BDS MB meetupov (Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije, sekcija Maribor), prišli do odločitve, da potrebujemo redni termin. Nekateri ste to že nekaj časa govorili, sedaj pa je prišlo do obroditve teh sadov. Bitcoin srečanja v Mariboru se bodo od slej dogajala vsak četrti četrtek v mescu, kar poenostavi, da si termin zapomnite in si ga je enostavno shraniti v koledar za vnaprej. Datumi so od sedaj naprej določeni vnaprej.

**ENG: August 27, 2026 – BDS MB Meetup #32 – Tracking Cash**

This time, we will take a look at how the fiat system tracks cash, which for now we generally consider to be the most anonymous form of payment. We will explore the technologies that scan cash every day and what kind of information can be derived from the collected data.
We will meet on **Thursday, August 27, at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava, Ulica Eve Lovše 15.**

The event is kindly hosted at NiceHash's premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.

If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.
Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) or on the

Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS.](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/)

Our friends from Bitcoin Shop will also be joining us – if you’d like to buy a hardware wallet or any other Bitcoin-related product, it can be conveniently delivered to Maribor.

**News from March 2026**
In March 2026, after more than two years of organizing BDS MB meetups (Bitcoin Association of Slovenia – Maribor chapter), we decided that we needed a regular schedule. Some of you had been suggesting this for quite some time, and now the idea has finally come to fruition.
From now on, Bitcoin meetups in Maribor will take place every fourth Thursday of the month, making the date easier to remember and allowing you to add future meetups to your calendar well in advance. The dates are now set ahead of time.
