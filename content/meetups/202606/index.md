# 25\. junij 2026 \- BDS MB meetup \#30 \- Bitcoin PlebWalk

## Bitcoin sprehod

Že nekaj časa sem se zanimal kaj točno so bitcoin plebwalki, ki sem jih zasledil po sosednjih državah. Nedolgo nazaj sem se ga udeležil v Grazu, tokrat, pa ga izvajamo tudi pri nas. Prvič v Sloveniji. Izbral sem štart v Koblarjevem zalivu in “obračališče” v Skif baru v Brestrnici. Pot je večinoma po makadamu dolžine \~4km ob Dravi. V primeru dežja se prestavimo na pivo na standardni lokaciji kavarne hotela Betnava.
Dobimo se v **četrtek 25. junija, ob 19. uri v Koblarjevem zalivu.**

**Danilo**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje **NiceHash**, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si)

### **ENG:** Bitcoin Walk

For some time, I had been curious about Bitcoin plebwalks that I had seen taking place in neighboring countries. Not long ago, I attended one in Graz, and now we are bringing the concept to Slovenia for the first time.
I have chosen **Koblarjev zaliv** as the starting point and **Skif Bar in Bresternica** as the turnaround point. The route is mostly along a gravel path beside the Drava River and is approximately 4 km long.
In case of rain, we will move the meetup to our usual location at the café of Hotel Betnava for a beer and conversation.
We will meet on **Thursday, June 25, at 7:00 PM at Koblarjev zaliv.**

**Danilo**

The event is kindly hosted at **NiceHash's** premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.
If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.
Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) or on the Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Our friends from **Bitcoin Shop** will also be joining us – if you’d like to buy a **hardware wallet** or any other Bitcoin-related product, it can be conveniently delivered to Maribor.
