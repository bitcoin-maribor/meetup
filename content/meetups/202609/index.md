# **24\. september 2026 \- BDS MB meetup \#33 \- Sokratski seminar**

## **Sokratski seminar**

Tokrat bomo na BDS MB prvič preizkusili nekoliko drugačen format srečanja – **Sokratski seminar**.

Namesto klasičnega predavanja bomo skupaj odprli nekaj aktualnih Bitcoin tem in se o njih pogovarjali skozi vprašanja, razlage in različne poglede. Moderator bo pripravil teme in skrbel, da se pogovor premika naprej, glavni del pa bomo ustvarili udeleženci sami. Svoje teme lahko prispevate tukaj: [https://docs.simonbuenemann.org/p/2026-09-BDS-MB-sokratski-seminar](https://docs.simonbuenemann.org/p/2026-09-BDS-MB-sokratski-seminar)
Pogledali bomo nekaj zanimivega dogajanja iz zadnjih tednov – od razvoja Bitcoina in Lightninga do zasebnosti, regulacije, novih orodij in drugih aktualnih vprašanj.
Ni treba, da si strokovnjak ali razvijalec. Namen seminarja je predvsem, da skupaj razmišljamo, vprašamo stvari, ki nam niso jasne, ter drug od drugega izvemo kaj novega.
Če se format obnese, ga bomo lahko v prihodnje še kdaj ponovili.

Dobimo se v **četrtek 24. septembra, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje **NiceHash**, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://bitcoin.si/) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si/)

### ENG: September 24, 2026 – BDS MB Meetup #33 – Socratic Seminar

## Socratic seminar

This time, at BDS MB, we will try a slightly different meetup format for the first time – a **Socratic Seminar**.

Instead of a traditional lecture, we will open up several current Bitcoin topics and discuss them through questions, explanations, and different perspectives. The moderator will prepare the topics and keep the discussion moving, while the participants themselves will create the main part of the seminar. You can contribute your own topics here: [https://docs.simonbuenemann.org/p/2026-09-BDS-MB-sokratski-seminar](https://docs.simonbuenemann.org/p/2026-09-BDS-MB-sokratski-seminar)

We will look at some interesting developments from the past few weeks – from Bitcoin and Lightning development to privacy, regulation, new tools, and other current topics.
You do not need to be an expert or a developer. The main purpose of the seminar is to think together, ask about things that are unclear to us, and learn something new from one another.
If the format works well, we may repeat it again in the future.

We will meet on **Thursday, September 24, at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava, Ulica Eve Lovše 15.**

The event is kindly hosted at **NiceHash's** premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.
If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.

Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](http://bitcoin.si/) or on the Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

Our friends from **Bitcoin Shop** will also be joining us – if you’d like to buy a **hardware wallet** or any other Bitcoin-related product, it can be conveniently delivered to Maribor.
