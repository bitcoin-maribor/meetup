**Kvantni računalniki (in Bitcoin)**

**Novica:**
V *preteklem mescu (marca 2026)* smo po dobrih dveh letih znotraj organiziranja BDS MB meetupov (Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije, sekcija Maribor), prišli do odločitve, da potrebujemo redni termin. Nekateri ste to že nekaj časa govorili, sedaj pa je prišlo do obroditve teh sadov. Bitcoin srečanja v Mariboru se bodo od slej dogajala **vsak četrti četrtek v mescu**, kar poenostavi, da si termin zapomnite in si ga je enostavno shraniti v koledar za vnaprej. Datumi so od sedaj naprej določeni vnaprej. Po tej shemi je bil že prejšnji meetup (marčevski) in sedaj tudi naslednji.

**Kvantni računalniki in Bitcoin:**

Tokrat nam bo **dr. Vladimir Hozjan** predstavil tematiko. Tako nas vabi:
Vsak teden v novicah berem o nekem novem mejniku kvantnih računalnikov — in vsakič se mi postavi vprašanje, koliko je v tem resnice in kdaj bo to res postal problem za moj Bitcoin. Na predavanju bomo skupaj pogledali, kaj kvantni računalniki danes zares zmorejo in kaj ne, katera tretjina vseh Bitcoinov že zdaj čaka na prvi dovolj zmogljiv stroj, in zakaj bi se lahko Q-Day zgodil že znotraj tega desetletja.
Dobimo se v **četrtek 28. maja, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**

Dogajanje nam v svojih prostorih omogoča podjetje **NiceHash**, za kar smo izjemno hvaležni.
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si)

**ENG: Quantum computers and Bitcoin**

## News (old)

In the past month, after more than two years of organizing BDS MB meetups (Bitcoin Association of Slovenia – Maribor chapter), we decided it was time to establish a fixed schedule. Some of you have been suggesting this for a while, and now it has finally come to fruition. From now on, Bitcoin meetups in Maribor will take place every fourth Thursday of the month, making it easier to remember and plan ahead. The dates are now predictable—this format was already used for the previous meetup and will continue for future ones.

## Quantum computers and Bitcoin

This time, **dr. Vladimir Hozjan** will present the topic. He invites us as follows:
Every week I read about a new milestone in quantum computing, and each time I wonder how much of it is true, and when it will actually become a problem for my Bitcoin. During the talk, we'll look together at what quantum computers can truly do today and what they can't, why one-third of all Bitcoin is already waiting for the first sufficiently powerful machine, and why Q-Day could happen within this decade.
We'll meet on **Thursday, May 28, at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava on Ulica Eve Lovše 15.**
The event is kindly hosted at **NiceHash's** premises, and we are extremely grateful for their support.
If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.
