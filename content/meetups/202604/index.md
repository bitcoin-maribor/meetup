# Bitcoin posojila

**NOVICA:**
V preteklem mescu smo po dobrih dveh letih znotraj organiziranja BDS MB meetupov (Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije, sekcija Maribor), prišli do odločitve, da potrebujemo redni termin. Nekateri ste to že nekaj časa govorili, sedaj pa je prišlo do obroditve teh sadov. Bitcoin srečanja v Mariboru se bodo od slej dogajala vsak četrti četrtek v mescu, kar poenostavi, da si termin zapomnite in si ga je enostavno shraniti v koledar za vnaprej. Datumi so sedaj naprej določljivi, po tej shemi je bil že prejšnji meetup in sedaj tudi naslednji.

## Bitcoin posojila

Vabljeni na predstavitev Bitcoin Posojila — praktičen vodič za tiste, ki imajo BTC in ne želijo prodajati. **Matjaž Lipuš** nam bo predstavil, kako z **zastavitvijo bitcoina** pridobiti likvidnost v fiat ali stablecoinih, hkrati pa **ohraniti izpostavljenost rasti**. Pregledali bomo ključne pojme (LTV, rehipotekacija, tveganja), primerjali vodilne platforme ter skupaj izračunali realne stroške posojila. Pridružite se nam in odkrijte strategijo, ki jo bogataši uporabljajo že stoletja — zastaviti, ne prodati. Dobimo se v **četrtek 23. aprila, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.**
Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si)

## News

In the past month, after more than two years of organizing BDS MB meetups (Bitcoin Association of Slovenia – Maribor chapter), we decided it was time to establish a fixed schedule. Some of you have been suggesting this for a while, and now it has finally come to fruition. From now on, Bitcoin meetups in Maribor will take place every fourth Thursday of the month, making it easier to remember and plan ahead. The dates are now predictable—this format was already used for the previous meetup and will continue for future ones.

## Bitcoin Loans

You are invited to a presentation on Bitcoin Loans — a practical guide for those who hold BTC and prefer not to sell. **Matjaž Lipuš** will explain how to **use Bitcoin as collateral** to access liquidity in fiat or stablecoins **while maintaining exposure to potential upside**. We will cover key concepts (LTV, rehypothecation, risks), compare leading platforms, and calculate the real costs of such loans together. Join us and discover a strategy the wealthy have used for centuries — borrow against your assets, don’t sell them.
We will meet on **Thursday, April 23 at 7:00 PM at the café of Hotel Betnava, Ulica Eve Lovše 15**.
If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.
Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) or on the Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
