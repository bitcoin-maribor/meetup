## **Strojne denarnice - pregled 2025**

Prisrčno vabljeni na že 23. srečanje Bitcoin Društva Slovenije - mariborske sekcije!
Tokrat se dobimo v **ponedeljek, 1. decembra, ob 19. uri v kavarni hotela Betnava na Ulici Eve Lovše 15.** Na srečanju bomo na hitro pregledali **novosti pri hardverskih denarnicah** oziroma podpisnikih za Bitcoin transakcije, o čemer bo predaval Elvis Požek iz Eventus Sistemi.
Če si kdo želi, lahko že vnaprej naroči denarnico prek [bitcoin-shop.si](http://bitcoin-shop.si), kjer trenutno poteka Black Friday akcija, in Elvis jo bo prinesel na dogodek.

**Skratka, vidimo se kmalu in se veselimo druženja ob manjši pogostitvi, ki jo prijazno zagotavlja NiceHash.**

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate na [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) ali se včlanite v društvo in z **članarino** podprete naša prizadevanja.
Več o Bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) ali na slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Z nami bodo tudi prijatelji iz **Bitcoin Shopa** – če bi kdo želel kupiti **strojno denarnico** ali drug Bitcoin produkt, je mogoče naročilo prevzeti kar v Mariboru.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Strojne denarnice in Bitcoin oblačila](https://bitcoin-shop.si)

***

### **ENG:** Hardware Wallets – 2025 Overview

You are warmly invited to the 23rd meetup of the Bitcoin Society of Slovenia – Maribor chapter.
We will gather on **Monday, December 1st, at 19:00 in the café of Hotel Betnava**, located at Ulica Eve Lovše 15. This time, we will take a short look at the latest developments in **Bitcoin hardware wallets** and signing devices. The presentation will be given by **Elvis Požek** from Eventus Sistemi.
If anyone would like to order a hardware wallet in advance, you can do so via [bitcoin-shop.si](http://bitcoin-shop.si), where the Black Friday promotion is still ongoing. Elvis will bring the ordered devices to the event.

**We look forward to seeing you there and to enjoying a small reception kindly provided by NiceHash.**

If you wish to support future Bitcoin-related events, you can donate at [https://bitcoin.si/donacije](https://bitcoin.si/donacije) or become a member of our society.
Read more about Bitcoin at [BITCOIN.SI](http://BITCOIN.SI) or on the Slovenian section of [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
Our friends from **Bitcoin Shop** will also be joining us – if you’d like to buy a **hardware wallet** or any other Bitcoin-related product, it can be conveniently delivered to Maribor.
➡️ [Bitcoin Shop – Hardware wallets and Bitcoin apparel](https://bitcoin-shop.si)
