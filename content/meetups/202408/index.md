**Vprašanja udeležencev**

**Na 11. meetupu skupine Bitcoin Maribor** se bomo poslužili novega koraka. Tokrat boste vi sokreatorji tematike. Za lažjo postavljanje vprašanj smo ustvarili odprto anketo. Vanjo vnesite vaša vprašanja na kateri si želite odgovor oziroma debato.
[https://forms.gle/yVWesieskX2QuTHT9](https://forms.gle/yVWesieskX2QuTHT9)

Srečanje se bo odvilo v četrtek, **29\. augusta\, ob 19h v kavarni Hotela Betnava** (ulica Eve Lovše 15, Maribor).
Pridružite se nam in razširite svoje znanje ter izkušnje v svetu napredne digitalne valute!

Nato se bomo družili v kavarni, kjer bo mogoče plačati z uporabo ligtning bitcoina. Za tiste ki bodo želeli bomo v pomoč pri prvi uporabi Bitcoin LightNing (LN) tehnologije in uporabi LN denarnice

Če nas želite podpreti in omogočiti prihodnje dogodke na temo Bitcoina, lahko donirate [tukaj](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/) prav tako se lahko včlanite v društvo in z članarino podprete naša prizadevanja.

Več o bitcoinu si lahko preberete tudi na [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) ali slovenskem delu [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).

**Questions from the participants**

**At the 11th meetup of the Bitcoin Maribor** group, we will try something new. We will take questions from the participants of the meetup. For that we have set up a form, to take the questions: https://forms.gle/yVWesieskX2QuTHT9.

After the event, we will gather at a café where you can meet other members of the Slovenian Bitcoin community and try out how Bitcoin payments work through the lightning network.

The meeting will take place on **Thursday, August 29, at 7 PM at the café in Hotel Betnava** (Eve Lovše Street 15, Maribor).

Meetup is hosted by **Bitcoin Društvo Slovenije** and company **NiceHash**.

If you want to support us and enable future Bitcoin events you can donate [here](https://bitcoin.si/donacije/).

If you want to know more, you can read our blog on [BITCOIN.SI](https://bitcoin.si) or on Slo section [EUROPEAN BITCOINERS](https://europeanbitcoiners.com/tag/slov/).
